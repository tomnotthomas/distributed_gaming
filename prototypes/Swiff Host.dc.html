Swiff Host, round 7. Calmer plates, anchored dials, a simpler share-until

Host on the PC

The round-4 app, kept as it was in structure and greys, and made more alive with restrained colour: a green family for standing and progress, the screen's game faintly tinting each instrument plate, and full-colour art wherever a game is the subject. It now carries everything from round 5: the demand ranking, protected sessions, reliability, a rate built from it, and levels.

  • Round 7: every instrument plate breathes with one slow gradient; no dial floats; share until is four clear choices and an exact time.
  • Rail: This PC, Games, Go live, Get paid, with your reliability and level at its foot on every screen after the first.
  • Choose your games is ranked by demand across Swiff, with Install on Steam for popular games you don't have.
  • Sessions are protected until they end; ending early is a separate, costly choice with a 5 minute warning for the player.
  • Your rate is built in the open: hardware rate, reliability, level.
