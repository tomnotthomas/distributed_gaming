Renter prototype. Paper Band, Reticle launch

Swiff v7

Full-colour key art in black, white and paper chrome, with lime only as a mark. The wall leads with one game and an instrument column; the game page ranks the machines and launches with a press-and-hold reticle; Ignition runs a dial through the four launch steps.

  • Point at or tab to Resume on the signed-in wall: its tick ring spins up and a short arc orbits the other way, then both ease to a stop.
  • Game page: crosshair reticle, with its own attention cue; press and hold fills it over 1.2 s, release drains it, a full hold shows Launching.
  • Ignition runs through all four steps on a 12 s loop: arc, lime head, needle, percentage and step legend move together.
  • Share your PC, in every top bar: signed out it opens the estimate (pick a tier, open How we got this number, drag the sliders); signed in as a host it opens the read-only earnings page. Setup and payout details stay in the desktop app.
  • Everything that moves on its own stops under reduced motion; the hold still works.
