# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Recorded from the repository and the launch set without an interview round (the redesign ran unattended);
every fact below is inferred from those sources, not confirmed in conversation.

## Platform

web

## Users

Players in a crew of friends who want to play their Steam games, including on a Mac, on a gaming PC that
someone in the crew (or another player) makes available. They arrive through Steam sign-in, usually in the
evening, wanting to be in a game within a minute.

## Product Purpose

Lanterel streams the player's own Steam games from a friend's or another player's idle gaming PC. The web
app after sign-in is where a player picks a game, sees which PC can run it and how well, holds to launch,
waits through the connection, plays, and comes back to a running session after a drop.

## Positioning

One gaming PC, the whole crew around it: the PCs are the players' own (crew PCs and shared PCs), not a
data centre, and the games are the ones the player already owns on Steam.

## Operating Context

A laptop or desktop browser at home, often a Mac; sometimes a phone. Sign-in is Steam's own (no password,
no account). The app keeps its own Paper Band look (`DESIGN.md`); the crew page and the crew invite keep
the launch set's light lobby look under the app's dark bar.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Screens: the game wall, a game's page with the ranked machines and hold-to-launch, the connecting screen
  (Ignition, including the Steam sign-in code on a rental PC), the live session, coming back to a session
  or queue, a lost machine, the profile and settings, Share your PC.
- Terminology and language rules: `data/swiff-landing-design/research/invite-pages/GLOSSARY.md` (German
  fully German, English fully English, one form per term).
- The brand name is a single token (Lanterel); renaming the product in the app is its own change.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Lanterel.
- The app's look is the Paper Band look it had before #107 (`DESIGN.md`). The light look #107 brought in on
  2026-10-07 is superseded: the captain asked the same day to restore the previous look. The crew page and
  invite keep the launch set's light lobby look, which the captain chose for them.
- Lime is a mark, never a fill.

## Evidence on Hand

Game key art and trailers from Steam (publisher-owned); no testimonials, prices beyond the Share your PC
estimate, or usage numbers exist, and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. In a game fast: every screen points at the one next action.
2. Say what is true about the machine: latency, time free, who owns it.
3. One product: the app, the launch pages and the crew pages carry the same name, words and marks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operable throughout (hold-to-launch works with Space and Enter), reduced motion respected, text
contrast at WCAG AA.
