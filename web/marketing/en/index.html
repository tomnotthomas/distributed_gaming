Questions about Steam on a Mac

Can I play Windows games on my Mac?

Yes, with {{brand}}, including Steam games that only exist for Windows. The game runs on a gaming PC in Germany and streams to your browser, so your Mac doesn't do the heavy lifting.

Do I need to install anything?

No. All you need is Chrome and a steady internet connection. We're still testing Safari.

Which Mac do I need?

Any Mac that runs a current version of Chrome, from a MacBook Air or MacBook Neo to a Mac mini. A steady connection matters more: use a cable, or sit close to the router.

Which Steam games can I play on my Mac?

With {{brand}}, most of your library, including Windows-only games. Rule of thumb: if it runs on the Steam Deck, it usually runs on {{brand}}. Games with kernel-level anti-cheat, like PUBG, Apex Legends or Call of Duty, don't.

Is my Steam account safe?

You sign in by scanning a QR code with the Steam app, so you never type your password anywhere. During your session the gaming PC runs a locked-down system, and it restarts when you're done. Your login is never stored on it.

What does {{brand}} cost?

Free during the beta. After that, at most €2 an hour, no subscription required.

Whose PC am I playing on?

At first on the gaming PC of a friend who invites you, or of a Founding Host in Germany: someone who shares their PC. For your session it runs {{brand}} OS, a separate system, and restarts when you're done. Your Steam login is never stored on it.

Do I have to scan the QR code every time?

Yes, once per session, with the Steam app on your phone. That's on purpose: your login never stays on someone else's PC, it's wiped when the session ends.

Do my saves carry over?

Yes, through Steam Cloud, just like on any other PC. Saves that don't use Steam Cloud, and mods, don't carry over, because every guest gets a fresh machine.

What if no PC is free?

You join the queue and get connected as soon as one frees up; just keep the page open. During the beta we only invite as many people as there are PCs.

Isn't this just Steam Remote Play Together?

Not quite. Remote Play Together streams the host's game, and you join its local multiplayer. With {{brand}}, you run your own game on your own Steam account on your friend's PC, so it works even with games they don't own.
